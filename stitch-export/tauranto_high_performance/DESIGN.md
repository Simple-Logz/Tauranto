## Brand & Style
The design system follows a **Corporate / Modern** aesthetic with a specific focus on high-utility SaaS for hospitality. It is characterized by high-contrast legibility, generous whitespace, and a mission-critical feel that ensures reliability in fast-paced restaurant environments.

## Colors
Use Canvas Cream as the global background, Surface White for cards, Sage Line for borders, Deep Leaf Emerald for primary actions, Service Lavender for AI voice states, and Harvest Saffron for warnings.

## Typography
The system relies on **Nunito Sans** with amplified weights 800 and 900 for high-glare restaurant environments.

## Layout & Spacing
Mobile uses 16px side margins and stacked content. Desktop/tablet uses 24px margins with multi-column card grids.

## Elevation & Depth
Hierarchy uses tonal layers and low-contrast outlines. Floating docks and modals use frosted glass treatment.

## Shapes
Standard cards use rounded-xl, buttons and inputs rounded-lg, navigation dock rounded-full, and status indicators circular.

## Components
Primary buttons are emerald with white 900-weight text and 56-62px height. Operational tiles use top-border accent colors. KPI cards stack icon, stat, and eyebrow label. Floating navigation uses frosted white. Form fields are at least 56px tall. Voice status uses Green (Standby), Tomato (Recording), Lavender (Thinking).
